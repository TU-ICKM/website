## About this website

This is the website of the International Conference on Knowledge Management (ICKM). The conference has been held since 2004 in cities on five continents.

## Background videos

The moving backgrounds on the home page and the History page were generated with artificial intelligence. They show abstract motifs. They do not show real people, real places or real events.
